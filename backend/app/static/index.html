Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — Thin-film wave interference across the surface of the medium. Microscopic layer thickness shifts perceived hue as incident light angle varies.

Index of refraction — The ratio of light's vacuum velocity to its phase speed within the dielectric material, governing Snell's boundary deviation and Fresnel reflection.

Dispersion — Spectral chromatic separation arising from wavelength-dependent refractive indices, splitting white illumination into prismatic fringes.

Transmission — Coherent propagation of radiant energy through bulk dielectric volume without absorptive extinction or diffuse scatter.

Roughness — Microfacet distribution variance defining the transition from specular mirror coherence to frosted sub-surface diffusion.
